## Executive Summary & Business Impact
* **Total Annual Economic Value Generated:** **$40.8M – $57.2M**
* **Predictive Churn Performance:** Supervised Random Forest achieves **PR-AUC = 0.718** and **7.2x Top-Decile Lift** (capturing 72% of impending churn within the top 10% risk group).
* **Proactive Hardware Replacement (EXP-2026-HW01):** Dispatches replacement hardware to subscribers experiencing $>3.5\%$ dish obstruction (a **2.50x hazard ratio increase**), achieving an **exact payback period of 3.5 months**.
* **Regional Lite Downsell Yield Engine (EXP-2026-PR02):** Converts price-sensitive emerging market accounts ($\text{PED} = -1.65$) facing dunning failure to a $20/mo tier, capturing **18.5% of churn-intent accounts**.
